Community Guidelines

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Stammtisch is built for recurring real-world gatherings. These guidelines explain the behavior we expect when people create groups, invite others, upload photos, suggest places, vote, RSVP, and meet in person.

Be Respectful

  • Treat other users, organizers, guests, and venue staff with respect.
  • Do not harass, threaten, bully, shame, impersonate, or discriminate against others.
  • Do not use Stammtisch to pressure people into events, purchases, relationships, or unwanted contact.

Keep Groups Safe And Lawful

  • Do not create groups or events for illegal, dangerous, exploitative, hateful, or deceptive purposes.
  • Do not share private addresses, sensitive personal information, or location details unless everyone involved understands and agrees.
  • Confirm reservations, accessibility needs, age restrictions, and venue rules directly with the venue when needed.

Use Photos And Content Responsibly

  • Only upload photos or content you own or have permission to use.
  • Do not upload explicit, violent, hateful, misleading, or invasive content.
  • Respect requests to remove photos or content that identify someone who does not want to be shown.

No Spam Or Platform Abuse

  • Do not spam invitations, scrape data, automate abuse, or attempt to bypass security controls.
  • Do not create fake accounts, fake groups, misleading venue listings, or deceptive promotions.
  • Do not interfere with the availability, security, or integrity of the Service.

Reporting Problems

Report abusive users, unsafe groups, inappropriate photos, privacy concerns, or platform misuse to support@stammtisch.pro. For privacy-specific requests, contact privacy@stammtisch.pro.

How We May Respond

We may remove content, restrict visibility, suspend or delete accounts, preserve evidence, contact users, or cooperate with lawful requests if we believe these guidelines or our Terms have been violated.

Related Policies

These guidelines supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
